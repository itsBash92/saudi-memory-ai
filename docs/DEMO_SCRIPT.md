# Demo Script

Suggested duration: 90–120 seconds.

## 1. The problem — 15 seconds

“Every street, home, and landmark holds a memory, but images are scattered and stories disappear over time. Saudi Memory AI connects place, image, personal account, and source.”

## 2. Discovery — 30 seconds

1. Open the home page.
2. Select **Try place recognition**.
3. Start the scan simulation.
4. Show the **Al Masmak Palace** result, confidence score, and short story.
5. Explain that the production flow uses image content and approximate location, then retrieves supporting evidence.

## 3. Contribution and trust — 30 seconds

“A user can add a family photo, its story, an approximate date, and an optional source. The system removes sensitive metadata, checks for duplicates, and shows review state and evidence clearly.”

## 4. Engagement and impact — 20 seconds

“Each trustworthy contribution strengthens the shared record and can earn points or a badge. Partner rewards can be connected later with anti-fraud safeguards.”

## 5. Closing — 10 seconds

“Saudi Memory AI is more than a guide to places. It is a national memory, preserved through local voices and made accessible to future generations.”

## Demo fallback

- Use the built-in illustrative data if connectivity is unavailable.
- Open `/api/health` to confirm the deployment is responding.
- Do not depend on a live camera during the first demo before permissions and the production model are complete.
