# Product Vision and MVP Scope

## Vision

Make **Saudi Memory AI** the trusted interface for discovering the memories of Saudi places and preserving local accounts through an experience that combines artificial intelligence with responsible community participation.

## Users

- A citizen or resident who wants to understand the story of a nearby place.
- An older adult or family preserving an old photo and its account.
- A visitor seeking an experience deeper than a conventional guide.
- A researcher or cultural organization that needs structured, traceable material.
- A content reviewer resolving disputed or uncertain contributions.

## User problem

Official information is fragmented, while personal memories remain scattered across phones and family albums. No simple experience consistently connects image, place, time, account, and source.

## Initial product scope

### In scope

- Place discovery and search.
- A prototype camera flow for landmark recognition.
- Place pages containing story, imagery, timeline, memories, and sources.
- Memory submission with media, text, approximate date, and an optional reference.
- Authentication, saved places, and favorites.
- Confidence scores and `draft / pending / verified / needs context / rejected` states.
- Internal points and badges.
- A focused moderation dashboard.

### Out of scope for the first release

- Open recognition of every place in the Kingdom on day one.
- Direct cash payouts.
- Unsourced historical claims generated by a model.
- Facial recognition or person identification.
- Real reward redemption before a partnership and anti-fraud controls are in place.

## MVP success measures

| Measure | Initial target |
| --- | --- |
| Place discovery journey completion | 70% or higher |
| Completed contribution rate | 30% of registered users |
| Contributions resolved without manual escalation | 60% or higher |
| Published content carrying evidence or a source | 80% or higher |
| Time to reach a place story | Under 8 seconds in the demo environment |

## Primary demo story

A visitor stands in front of Al Masmak Palace, opens the camera, sees the place identified with a confidence score, explores a short story and timeline, then contributes an old family photo connected to the location and earns the **Memory Keeper** badge.
